### Q1

A father has four children, named Ram, Radha, Balu and Siva. He has an asset of 27 lakhs. He wanted to share it among his children. He wanted to give one share to Ram, Ravi and Siva, and 3 shares to Balu, as he is a differently abled child. He wanted to donate the left-out money to a charitable trust.

### Q2

A business man gave 25 lakhs to his son and advised him to start new business. The son started a new business and made 20 times as a profit in 10 years. The father is heading for a business meet urgently and wanted to know what is the profit earned by his son in these 10 years. Help the son to find the profit quickly.

**Marks:** 10 · Unit 3

### Q3
